# Pitch — Alertix AI

## One-liner
Alertix AI is real-time multi-hazard intelligence for India — earthquake, flood, cyclone, wildfire, landslide, and damage assessment — built on open data and deployable on a single server.

## Three differentiators

1. **Open-source, on-premise deployable.** State DMAs and insurers cannot pay watsonx prices. Alertix runs on a Rs 50,000 box.
2. **Multilingual citizen-report triage.** Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Bengali SOS messages — incumbents are English-only.
3. **Scope honesty.** We tell customers what we don't do. That wins enterprise trust faster than feature lists.

## Pilot targets (priority order)
1. General insurer with parametric flood or seismic products
2. State disaster management authority (Telangana SDMA closest)
3. Logistics or utility firm with weather-exposed operations

## The ask
Free 30-day pilot, on their data, with a measurable success metric defined up front.
