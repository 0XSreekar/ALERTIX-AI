# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

ALERTIX-AI is built to help people stay safe during disasters. We want the project around it to feel just as safe. Everyone who contributes here (regardless of experience level, background, location, or language) should be able to do so without harassment.

## Expected Behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith in code reviews and issue threads.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change, and explain reasoning when you decline one.
- Keep discussion in English where possible so the wider community can follow along.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Personal attacks, insults, or demeaning comments.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.
- Deliberately posting false hazard or emergency data.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and the wiki, as well as any time someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

If you see behaviour that breaks these rules, open a private security advisory or contact the maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may warn, temporarily block, or permanently ban anyone who violates this code.
